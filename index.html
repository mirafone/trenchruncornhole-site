Trench Run Cornhole

A 3D-printed, pinball-style, sensor-scored trench run cornhole board - your mission is to land the proton torpedoes into the exhaust port!

Cornhole meets pinball: a trench run diorama, 3D-printed tile by tile. Sensors catch every shot, and the board answers with lights and sound.

100+3D prints
9+ kgof filament
Pinball × cornhole

What it does

  • Sensor-activated: hits and misses are detected automatically
  • Exciting LED lighting and sound effects for every outcome
  • Plexiglass Death Star shield and hand-made bean bags
  • Web dashboard for game state and scoring, and audience trivia and mini game!
